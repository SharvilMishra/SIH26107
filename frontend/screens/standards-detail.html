1. Scope and Applicability

This standard applies to plugs and fixed or portable socket-outlets for a.c. only, with or without earthing contact, with a rated voltage above 50 V but not exceeding 250 V and a rated current not exceeding 16 A, intended for household and similar purposes, either indoors or outdoors.

check Included

  • Plugs incorporated in cord sets
  • Plugs and socket-outlets incorporated in appliances
  • Adapters (subject to specific clauses)

close Excluded

  • Industrial purposes
  • Extra-low voltage devices
  • Fuses or automatic switches (unless specifically incorporated)

Crucial Update in 2019 Revision

The 2019 revision phases out the older 5-pin multi-socket configurations and mandates shutters for all socket-outlets to enhance child safety.
